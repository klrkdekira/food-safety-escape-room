### **Q1 (MCQ): Continuous & Dispersed Phase**

In a mayonnaise system, which is correct?

A. Oil \= continuous phase, water \= dispersed phase  
 B. Water \= continuous phase, oil \= dispersed phase  
 C. Gas \= continuous phase, oil \= dispersed phase  
 D. Solid \= continuous phase, water \= dispersed phase

ANSWER ✔ **B. Water \= continuous phase, oil \= dispersed phase** *(typical O/W system like mayonnaise)*

### **Q7 (Matching): Emulsion Instability**

Match the instability:

* Creaming  
* Flocculation  
* Coalescence  
* Ostwald ripening  
* Phase inversion  
* Breaking

With:  
 A. Droplets fuse permanently  
 B. Reversible clustering of droplets  
 C. Droplets rise due to density difference  
 D. Growth of large droplets at expense of small ones  
 E. Continuous phase changes  
 F. Complete separation of phases

ANSWER: Creaming → **C**  
Flocculation → **B**  
Coalescence → **A**  
Ostwald ripening → **D**  
Phase inversion → **E**  
Breaking → **F**
